Feynos ComicReader

An Android reader for comics, manga and ebooks, with a Google Play Books–style page curl. Wworks on any Android 8.0+ phone or tablet.

## Features

### Formats
- **Comics:** CBZ, CBR (RAR 4 and earlier), ZIP and RAR comic archives
- **Documents:** PDF
- **Ebooks:** EPUB, MOBI / AZW / AZW3, FB2, TXT
- Format is detected from the file contents, so misnamed files still open
- DRM-protected books are not supported

### Bookshelf
- Add a whole folder; every subfolder becomes its own shelf, titled with its path
- Add single files too
- Rescan to pick up new and removed files while keeping reading progress
- Collapse a shelf by tapping its title; long-press a shelf to remove it
- "Continue reading" row with what you opened last
- Covers generated automatically, with format badges
- Percentage read on every cover, plus a progress bar
- **✓ READ** tag on finished books
- Menu (three bars, top left): **Bookshelf** (everything), **Read**, **Unread**, **Settings**
- Long-press a book to mark it read or unread (unread also resets it to page one), or remove it

### Reading comics, manga and PDFs
- Page curl animation, or plain sliding pages if you prefer (toggle in Settings)
- Only the page itself lifts; the dark margins stay put
- Sideways page pulling only: the page can't be dragged up or down
- Two-page spreads in landscape and on the Fold's unfolded screen, turning one page at a time, hinged at the spine
- Layout choices: Auto, single page, two pages with the cover alone, two pages with no cover gap
- Left-to-right and right-to-left (manga) reading direction
- Page slider, tap the screen edges or use the volume keys to turn pages
- Percentage read shown at the bottom right while reading

### Reading ebooks
- Books are laid out as real pages and turned with the same page curl as comics
- Two pages side by side on wide screens
- Adjustable text size; Light, Sepia and Dark themes, plus a quick dark mode toggle
- Table of contents and chapter list
- Your place is saved by position in the text, so it survives changes to font size and screen shape
- Pictures, headings, emphasis and justified, hyphenated text
- **Page header and footer:** choose book title, chapter, page number, "page X of Y", percent read, or your own note (kept per book)
- **Dictionary:** hold your finger on a word to see its definition (English, uses an internet connection, with a backup source, simple word-form matching and saved lookups so repeat words work offline). Or type a word to look it up.
- **Footnotes and links:** tap a footnote number to read the note in a popup, jump to it with a Back button to return, or follow other in-book links

### Bookmarks
- Star the current page to bookmark it; a ribbon shows on bookmarked pages
- Bookmark list per book: tap to jump, long-press to delete
- Works in comics, PDFs and ebooks

### Settings
- Defaults for page curl, reading direction, page layout, ebook text size, theme, page mode, header and footer
- Anything you change while reading a book applies to that book only; other books keep following the defaults
- Reset button to put the defaults back to the standard values

### Other
- Opens files from file managers and other apps (CBZ, CBR, PDF, EPUB and more)
- Dark interface with a purple "FC" book icon, including a themed (single-colour) icon for Android 13+
- No account, no ads, no tracking. Your books stay on your device. The only network use is the dictionary lookup.

## Known limits
- RAR5 .cbr files, DRM books and Huffman-compressed MOBI files can't be opened
- Ebooks always read left to right; right-to-left text layout isn't supported
- Tables and complex CSS layouts in ebooks aren't supported
- Links to websites inside ebooks do nothing
- Dictionary is English only and needs internet for new words
